---
title: The Old Front Gate
author: Paul Laurence Dunbar
source: The Saturday Evening Post, July 1, 1899
date: 2026-10-18
---

W’en daih’s chillun in de house,\
&emsp;Dey kin keep on gittin’ tall;\
But de folks don’ seem to see\
&emsp;Dat dey’s growin’ up at all,\
Twell dey fin’ out some fine day\
&emsp;Dat de gals has ’menced to grow,\
W’en dey notice ez dey pass\
&emsp;Dat de front gate’s saggin’ low.

W’en de hinges creak an’ cry,\
&emsp;An’ de bahs go slantin’ down,\
You kin reckon dat hit’s time\
&emsp;Fu’ to cas’ yo’ eye eroun’,\
’Cause daih ain’ no ’sputin’ dis,\
&emsp;Hit’s de trues’ sign to show\
Dat daih’s cou’tin’ goin’ on\
&emsp;W’en de ol’ front gate sags low.

Oh, you grumble an’ complain,\
&emsp;An’ you prop dat gate up right;\
But you notice right nex’ day\
&emsp;Dat hit’s in de same ol’ plight.\
So you fin’ dat hit’s a rule,\
&emsp;An’ daih ain’ no use to blow,\
W’en de gals is growin’ up,\
&emsp;Dat de front gate will sag low.

Den yo’ t’ink o’ yo’ young days,\
&emsp;W’en you cou’ted Sally Jane,\
An’ you so’t o’ feel ashamed\
&emsp;Fu’ to grumble an’ complain,\
’Cause yo’ ricerlection says,\
&emsp;An’ you know hits wo’ds is so,\
Dat huh pappy had a time\
&emsp;Wid his front gate saggin’ low.

So you jes’ looks on an’ smiles\
&emsp;At ’em leanin’ on de gate,\
Try’n’ to t’ink whut he kin say\
&emsp;Fu’ to keep him daih so late.\
But you lets dat gate erlone,\
&emsp;Fu’ yo’ ’sperunce goes to show\
Twell de gals is ma’ied off\
&emsp;It gwine keep on saggin’ low.
